# ไกด์อาชีพจากชุมชน

The **ไกด์อาชีพ** tool replaces the Skill Simulator menu entry. Its link is `#class-guide`; an old `#skill-simulator` link forwards there. All twelve class grids, three servers, normal/C/EX point rules and original skill frames are reused.

## For guide authors

Choosing **เขียนไกด์** asks how to start: **เริ่มไกด์ใหม่** (an empty build), **เขียนต่อ** (the draft saved in this browser; shown only when there is one) or **นำเข้าโค้ดไกด์** (paste a code). The editor then walks through six numbered steps, one at a time (the skill grid shows beside the step in 3, 4 and 5, where the step decides what a click on it does; the other steps take the whole page): 1 ข้อมูลไกด์, 2 สเตตัสและเซ็ต (the stats and sets editors right in the panel), 3 จัดบิลด์, 4 โน้ตสกิล, 5 หน้าปกและตรวจ (cover icons, preview), 6 บันทึกและส่ง (save the guide as a PNG or, on a computer, copy it as a picture with **คัดลอกรูป**, and / or copy the guide code and send it to jumpptty on Discord for the Bible's archive; it lists anything still missing, and a missing title, author or date sends you back to step 1). Steps 2 and 4 are optional (marked ไม่บังคับ on their tabs), a finished step shows ✓ on its tab, and the tabs move between steps; the step is remembered.

1. Open ไกด์อาชีพ and choose **เขียนไกด์** (Write a guide). The top row has two page tabs, **ไกด์จากผู้เล่น** and **เขียนไกด์**; the page you are on is gold. To start from an existing code, choose **นำเข้าโค้ดไกด์** at the top of the editor.
2. Fill in the guide title, author name, the **date** you wrote the guide (typed as วัน/เดือน/ปี, e.g. 16/10/2026, or picked from the calendar icon; never later than today, so a Buddhist-era year such as 2569 is refused with the hint วันที่ต้องไม่เกินวันนี้ (ปี ค.ศ. เช่น 2026)) (required; it replaces the old free-text game patch) and the build description. To emphasise text, select it and press **Ctrl+B** (Cmd+B on a Mac), as in a word processor: it turns bold gold, and a space is added before and after it when it touches other text. Pressing Ctrl+B on text that is already bold turns it back. The box placeholders say so; there is no toolbar button. Formatting is stored as `**text**` in the code and shown in previews and published guides. Pasted text is kept as plain text; Ctrl+Z inside the editor undoes text edits.
3. Choose the server, class and level, then build using the existing skill grid. Normal learning and undo (Ctrl+Z) remain available in **จัดบิลด์** mode; the **จัดบิลด์ / เขียนโน้ตสกิล** switch sits in the bar right above the trees.
4. Optionally fill **สเตตัสแนะนำ** (recommended stats): any of ATK, DEF, TAL, AGI, VIT, CHA, INT, LCK, each a whole number 0-512 (`250+` = or more), a share `1-100%` or one of the words **ตัน** (max it out) / **ตาม** (as needed); any mix (user 2026-10-18). The editor starts with no boxes: the dashed **+ เพิ่มสเตตัส** dial opens a picker of the stats not added yet, and each one becomes the same flask readers see, with the value typed straight into it (`180`, `250+`, `70%`; `050%` is tidied to `50%`), the **ตัน** / **ตาม** pills under it (press again to clear) and **×** to remove it; the + goes once all 8 are in. Drag a flask (anywhere on it, its input included; a plain click still types) to change the order; on a phone press and hold it for a moment first, since a quick swipe scrolls the page. Ctrl+Z undoes a ×, a ตัน / ตาม press or a reorder (typing keeps the box's own undo). A line under the flasks adds up the shares (`รวม % ทั้งหมด: N%`, green `✓ ครบ 100`, red `ยังขาด N` / `เกินมา N`) and flags a typo (a red ring); the shares of a guide must total 100, and no flask may be red, before it can be exported or saved as a picture (the editor goes to step 2 and says why), and step 6 lists either as missing. The stats show in the order the author added them. A blank stat is not stored and not shown to readers. Only the numbers drive the hover timings in the reader view (AGI, INT and CHA, with LCK); shares and words never do.
   Both sit in a dialog behind the **สเตตัสและเซ็ตแนะนำ** button (the button says what is filled in, e.g. สเตตัส 3, 2 เซ็ต). Optionally fill **เซ็ตแนะนำ** (recommended sets): up to three sets, each a weapon, armor, hat, trinket, boot and pet (set 1 = the first choice of every slot, set 2 the second, set 3 the third). Each pick opens a searchable list of the game items your class can use (with their in-game icons, in game order); a new item not in the list can be typed instead. Weapon, armor and hat picks can also name the item they are a **Replica** of and one stat to put in (**ยัด**, e.g. ยัด INT). Drag an item's icon up or down its own slot column to move it to another set (user 2026-10-18): the others in that column shift, its Replica and ยัด go with it, a set never ends up empty above a filled one, a plain click still opens the list, on a phone the icon needs a short press and hold (a swipe scrolls), and Ctrl+Z undoes the move. Only the item matters: no item stats are shown, since those get rebalanced.
5. Choose **เขียนโน้ตสกิล ★**, then click a skill to explain your choice. The note box opens where the description was (the description comes back when it closes). Notes can explain either selected or skipped skills. **ลบโน้ต** (or saving an empty note) removes its star, and Ctrl+Z brings it back. Each rank/cell has its own note.
   Choose **ไอคอนหน้าปก** (cover icons, required: คัดลอกโค้ดไกด์ asks for at least one first; the row shows five numbered circles that fill as you pick, in the same switch above the trees, or the ไอคอนหน้าปก row under the description): click up to five skills in the order you want them; the grid numbers them 1-5, clicking one again removes it, **ล้าง** clears them and Ctrl+Z undoes. They are the guide's picture on its gallery card, in the reader's title row and in the PNG (the class portrait is no longer used): one icon is one big circle, two sit side by side, three form a triangle (the note-icon ring).
6. Under the description, the finishing buttons are **ดูตัวอย่าง** (exactly what a visitor will see; **กลับไปแก้ไกด์** in the top row brings you back), **ดาวน์โหลดรูป** (saves the guide as a picture, see Reader interactions) and **คัดลอกโค้ดไกด์**.
7. Choose **คัดลอกโค้ดไกด์** (copy icon) to copy the guide code to the clipboard, then send it to the site maintainer. The code is short: `TG3` followed by Chinese characters (user 2026-10-20): the guide as compact binary (one bit per skill slot, Thai text at one byte per character), compressed when that helps, written 14 bits per character with a check character at the end. It is usually 30-960 characters (about 200 on average), fits in one Discord message, and Discord's formatting can't break it (TG2 codes lost `_` pairs to italics). Choose **นำเข้าโค้ดไกด์**, paste a code (`TG3`, an older `TG2.` or `12TG1.` code, or an old JSON code; a TG3 code may come with the chat text around it, and spaces, line breaks and backticks are ignored; a damaged one says to copy it again) into the small text box and press Enter to resume editing. An old code that has a patch but no date opens fine; export then asks for the date.

The draft is saved only in the author's current browser. Export the code to keep a portable copy. Creating a guide from the reader view starts an independent empty build or resumes the author's own draft; it does not edit an embedded expert guide.

## For the maintainer: embed submitted guides

**Guide manager, no Claude session (user 2026-10-20):** double-click `scripts/add_guide.cmd` (Desktop shortcut "12Tails Add Guide"); `node scripts/add_guide.js` serves a page on `http://127.0.0.1:4782` only (a start-up key on every request, Host and Origin checked) and opens it. Keep the console window open while using it. The page lists every guide (class face, views) with **แทนที่** and **ลบ**; pasting a code shows a live preview (the number it will get, a heads-up when a guide with the same title and author already exists, with a shortcut to แทนที่ it instead; uploading it as a new guide is allowed (user 2026-10-20), the Discord-italics `_` hint when it cannot be read). A code whose date is later than today (the validator refuses it; a Buddhist-era year like 20/10/2569 decodes to a wrapped 2031 date) or that has no date gets today's local date, shown on the date chip as "(แก้จาก …)" or "(โค้ดไม่มีวันที่ ใช้วันนี้)" (user 2026-10-20). **Batches (user 2026-10-20):** nothing is published straight away. ใส่ในรายการ (a pasted code: add, or replace after แทนที่) and ลบ put the change in the รายการที่จะอัป queue (planned numbers shown, one queued change per guide, ✕ to take one out, Ctrl+Z undoes queue edits first, kept in localStorage across reloads); **อัปขึ้น GitHub (N รายการ)** publishes the whole queue, or checks it with ตรวจอย่างเดียว. The changes are applied in queue order on one copy, so ids and tombstones see the earlier items, and an error names its item (รายการที่ N). Every publish runs one pipeline: refuse while `index.html` has uncommitted work, edit `CLASS_GUIDES`, prepend one changelog entry (also the commit subject: one change `feat(bible): class guide #N <server> <Class> <title> by <author>`, `fix(bible): class guide #N is updated: …`, `… is removed (…)`; a batch `feat(bible): class guides #47 …; #43 is updated: …; #45 is removed (…)`, `fix` when nothing is added), `validate_skills.js --full` once, one commit, push; a failed check puts `index.html` back, so a batch goes up whole or not at all. **Replace** keeps the id. **Remove** turns the line into `// #N removed <date>: <title> by <author>`, so the number is never given out again (new ids count past these notes). **Undo** (button, or Ctrl+Z once the queue history is empty) reverses the last publish made since start-up, the whole batch, through the same pipeline. Command line: `add <code>`, `replace <N> <code>`, `delete <N>`, with `--dry-run`, `--no-push`, `--yes`.

**Guide manager on the phone (user 2026-10-20: away from home, PC off):** the same page, `12t_projects/guide-manager/index.html`, is also served by GitHub Pages at `https://jumpptty.github.io/12tails/12t_projects/guide-manager/` (not linked from the Bible, `noindex`). There it works without the PC: it reads `index.html` through the GitHub API and publishes by starting `.github/workflows/guide-manager.yml`, which runs the same pipeline as the PC, one stage per workflow step (`node scripts/add_guide.js batch <request.json> --stage git|edit|check|commit|push`), so the page ticks the same steps (ส่งงานให้ GitHub first). Design: [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-guide-manager-mobile-design.md](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-guide-manager-mobile-design.md).
* **Pasted codes are untrusted** (review 2026-10-20): a code whose class or server is not a real one is refused (`decodeCode`, `gmCheckGuide`), and the page escapes every value it writes into HTML. Keep it that way when touching the page.
* **Key:** a fine-grained GitHub token, repository `12tails` only, **Actions: Read and write** (Metadata: Read-only comes with it). It lives in `localStorage` of the whole `jumpptty.github.io` site, which also serves the Bible and its GoatCounter script; the token's narrow scope is what limits the damage if something there read it. Pasted once per browser on the key screen and kept in that browser's `localStorage` (`12t-gm-key`), sent only to `api.github.com` (the page's CSP allows nothing else). The status pill opens **ลืมกุญแจ**. An expired or wrong key brings the key screen back with the reason; the queue is kept. The user's current key expires 2027-10-20.
* **Phones (≤ 760 px):** bottom tabs วางโค้ด / รายการที่จะอัป / ไกด์ทั้งหมด (layout B, picked from three mocks) and a publish bar above them while the queue has items; wider windows keep the PC layout.
* **What it reports back:** each run ends with one annotation, `gm-result` (subject, real numbers, commit, undo text) or `gm-error` (the Thai message, then the validator's lines). GitHub cuts an annotation at 4,096 characters, so `batch` keeps them under 4,000. A run that stops before the pipeline (checkout, setup) or is cancelled by the one-run-at-a-time queue shows a plain message and a link to the run.
* **Undo** reverses the last publish made from that browser, by its commit (`{"undo":{"sha":…}}`: every `CLASS_GUIDES` line the commit changed goes back, rebuilt in the standard form; an added guide becomes a tombstone). It refuses when a guide was changed after that publish ("ไกด์ #N ถูกแก้หลังจากนั้นแล้ว ย้อนกลับไม่ได้").
* **A run is never lost by the phone:** it is recorded (`12t-gm-pending`) before GitHub is asked; dropped requests are retried ("ต่อ GitHub ไม่ได้ กำลังลองใหม่…"); a reload, a sleeping phone or the network coming back picks it up again; publish and undo stay off meanwhile. If GitHub finished it but no result came back, the page says "ตรวจใน GitHub ก่อนอัปซ้ำ" with the link: check before publishing again, the guides may already be up. A finished publish takes out of the queue only what it sent.
* **Commits from the phone** have Jump as author and github-actions[bot] as committer, and reach GitHub first: **`git pull` before working on the PC.** Their push starts the Pages build by itself (checked 2026-10-20). If GitHub mails that "pages build and deployment" failed with "Timeout reached, aborting!" in the deploy job, that is GitHub's side: Actions → that run → Re-run jobs → Re-run failed jobs (seen once, 2026-10-20).
* **Validator checks** (`[GUIDE MANAGER ERROR]`): the page's class faces (`const FACES = …`, one line) must equal `CLASS_HERO_ICONS` in `assets.js` (after repainting portraits, write the line again from `require("./scripts/bible_assets.js").loadAssets().CLASS_HERO_ICONS`); the page's own code reader ("gm-core") must read every embedded guide's TG3 / TG2 / 12TG1 code exactly as `scripts/guide_code.js` does, and parse `CLASS_GUIDES` as `add_guide.js` does; its reading of GitHub's run, job and annotation replies is checked on recorded shapes. `node scripts/add_guide.js --selftest` runs the pipeline, the batch stages and undo-from-commit on throwaway repos.

Locate `const CLASS_GUIDES = [...]` in [index.html](index.html). Submissions arrive as short codes: turn each into its JSON object with `node scripts/guide_code.js decode <code>` (it also accepts a file path, and `encode <json file>` goes the other way), then add the **JSON objects** to the array, separated by commas:

```js
const CLASS_GUIDES = [
  // Paste the first exported JSON object here.
  // Add a comma between objects, then paste the next exported JSON object.
];
```

Paste the object itself, not a quoted JSON string, and do not include an outer array from another source. The guide's skill icons already live in the HTML; no image files need to accompany submissions. The tool remains a single offline HTML file.

**Every embedded guide needs a running number `"id"`** (a positive integer: 1, 2, 3 ... in the order guides were added; unique, never reused, never renumbered), written first in the object, e.g. `{"id":3,"format":"12t-class-guide",...}`. It is not part of the portable format (the importer ignores it); it makes the link `index.html#class-guide/<number>`, which opens that guide straight in the reader view, and the address bar already holds that link (click the guide title in the reader to copy it as a Discord heading). The reader's **คัดลอกโค้ดไกด์** button copies the guide's portable code instead; pasting it into **นำเข้าโค้ด** in the editor makes an editable copy. Never change a number after it was shared (a removed guide leaves a gap); the validator fails on a missing or duplicate number.

Only real submitted guides belong in this array. The list contains user-submitted guides; do not invent experts or builds. Valid guides appear in the community list with title, class, server, author and date. Invalid entries are excluded and the page reports how many could not open. Imported author codes never add entries to this array automatically.

Before publishing, run `node scripts/validate_skills.js`. Every embedded entry must pass the schema and existing simulator allocation rules. Changes to `index.html` still need its usual backup/checkpoint and matching changelog entry.

## Portable format, version 1

```json
{
  "format": "12t-class-guide",
  "version": 1,
  "title": "Guide title",
  "author": "Author name",
  "date": "2026-10-16",
  "description": "Build description.\nMultiple lines are supported.",
  "build": {
    "server": "tot",
    "classKey": "wolf",
    "level": 100,
    "learned": [101, 102, 103, 451]
  },
  "stats": { "agi": 120, "int": 96, "cha": 60, "lck": 40 },
  "gear": { "weapon": [{ "item": "w_pgn10", "replica": { "item": "w_pgn11" }, "stat": "int" }, { "name": "Staff of Tomorrow" }], "pet": [{ "item": "p_lcr" }] },
  "notes": {
    "103": "Why this skill rank is selected.",
    "201": "Why this skill is skipped."
  }
}
```

* `stats` (optional): the author's recommended stats, any of `atk`, `def`, `tal`, `agi`, `vit`, `cha`, `int`, `lck`. A value is a whole number 0-512 (0 is a real value), a string `"N%"` with N 1-100 and no leading zero (`"70%"`), or the string `"ตัน"` or `"ตาม"` (user 2026-10-18). **The key order is the display order** (the author's order); guides written before 2026-10-18 list their keys in the old fixed order, so they look the same. Only filled stats are stored, the key is left out when none is filled, and guides without it stay valid and export unchanged. An unknown key or any other value is rejected on import, and so is a finished guide whose `%` values do not total exactly 100 (`สเตตัสแนะนำแบบ % ต้องรวมได้ 100`; drafts may). Only the numbers feed the hover timings.
* `gear` (optional): `weapon`, `armor`, `hat`, `trinket`, `boot`, `pet`, each a list of 1-3 ranked picks. A pick is `{ "item": "<game id>" }` (from `GEAR_DATA`, read from WeaponData / ArmorData / AccessoryData / TrinketData / BootData / PetData; the item must be one the guide's class can use: its own, the shared `all` / `fem` / `mal` ones, every pet) or `{ "name": "<1-60 characters>" }` for an item not in the game data. Weapon / armor / hat picks may add `replica` (the item it is a Replica of, same two forms) and `stat` (one of the 8 stat keys). Empty slots are not stored. In the format 2 short code it is an optional block at the end, so codes without gear are unchanged.
* `cover` (required, user 2026-10-16; drafts may lack it): 1-5 cell ids (up to 3 before 2026-10-17) of the guide's grid (not placeholders), in the author's order; drafts drop ids the class or server no longer has. In the format 2 code it is an optional block (`0x49`, count, one slot byte each).
* `modes` (required for a finished guide, user 2026-10-20; drafts may lack it): what the guide is for, `["pve"]`, `["pvp"]` or `["pve", "pvp"]` (`CLASS_GUIDE_MODES` order). The editor asks for it in step 1 (ไกด์นี้สำหรับ PVE / PVP, both allowed); readers see PVE / PVP chips on the gallery card, in the guide's title row and in its PNG, and the gallery filters by it (ประเภท). In the format 2 code it is an optional block `0x4D` + one byte (bit 0 PVE, bit 1 PVP); format 1 uses key `m`. A code made before the tag existed reads without it: the guide manager (`scripts/add_guide.cmd`) shows PVE / PVP toggles to set it before publishing.
* `server`: `og` (BB), `tot` or `tto`. `classKey`: lowercase class name.
* `learned`: unique numeric cell IDs, including every learned lower rank and prerequisite; export sorts them. Cell IDs use the existing tree/row/column scheme. ToT EX cells are legal only on ToT.
* `notes`: cell ID strings mapped to text with optional `**bold gold**` emphasis. A note may target an unselected skill, but never an unknown or unpublished placeholder cell.
* Required for export: title (up to 160 characters), author (120), at least one of PVE / PVP (`modes`) and `date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, a real calendar day from 2000 on and no later than today; user 2026-10-20: two codes typed 20/10/2569, a Buddhist-era year, which the 2-byte day count wrapped to 2031-07-05. The editor and its calendar stop at the author's today; the shared check allows up to tomorrow in UTC so no time zone makes a fresh guide look future. A draft or an imported code with a later date opens without it, so the author types it again). Description allows 12,000 characters; each note allows 3,000. The complete code allows 131,072 characters. Import never executes code or renders HTML from submissions.
* `date` is the day the author wrote the build; readers see it as `16 ต.ค. 2026`. It does not select historical mechanics; allocation validation uses the tool's current rules for that server. Update the rules and guide data together when a server changes requirements. A `patch` key from an old code is ignored.
* **Short code, format 3** (`TG3`, what exports write since 2026-10-20; Discord ate `_` pairs in TG2 codes as italics): the format 2 bytes below (flag byte + binary), bits in order, 14 per character as U+4E00 + n, zero-padded, then one check character (CRC-16/CCITT of the bytes, low 14 bits). Bit 1 of the flag byte says the padding held a whole extra byte, which the reader drops. Reading takes `TG3` anywhere in the pasted text and ignores everything outside U+4E00-U+8DFF. The page (`classGuideToCjk` / `classGuideFromCjk`) and `scripts/guide_code.js` (`toCjk` / `fromCjk`) write the same characters; the validator checks every length 1-60 bytes, the round trip, a pasted chat message and a changed character.
* **Short code, format 2** (`TG2.`, written until 2026-10-20 and still imported; `guide_code.js encode2` writes one): base64url of a flag byte (0 stored, 1 deflate-raw) + binary: server and class in one byte (class order rabbit mole bat bison cat chameleon monkey panda penguin sheep whale wolf; server og tot tto), level, the learned skills as 20 bytes (bit `(tree-1)*40 + row*4 + (col-1)`), the date as 2 bytes of days since 2000-01-01 (both encoders throw on a date the 2 bytes cannot hold rather than wrap it), a stats mask byte + 2 bytes per stat (a number 0-512 with bit 15 = `250+`, bit 14 + a share 1-100 = `N%`, or bit 13 + a word index 0 ตัน / 1 ตาม; user 2026-10-18), then title, author, description and each note (slot byte + text) as length-prefixed text where ASCII and Thai (U+0E00-0E5B) take one byte and anything else is `0xDC` + its code point. A guide whose stats are not in the fixed order adds an optional block `0x4F`, count, one stat index per stat in the author's order (a guide in the fixed order writes no block, so codes made before 2026-10-18 read and re-encode exactly as before). A guide with a PVE / PVP tag ends with the mode block `0x4D` + one byte (bit 0 PVE, bit 1 PVP; user 2026-10-20). The page (`classGuideBinary` / `classGuideFromBinary`, `classGuidePack` / `classGuideUnpack`) and `scripts/guide_code.js` write identical bytes; the validator checks it.
* **Short code, format 1** (`12TG1.`, earlier exports, still imported): the compact form `{t, a, d, x, s, c, l, k, v, n}` (plus `g` gear, `i` cover, `m` modes when set) as minified JSON, deflate-raw, base64url (`node scripts/guide_code.js` decodes both; `encode1` writes it).

## Reader interactions

* **ความครบถ้วนของเนื้อหา stars (user 2026-10-20, gallery cards only):** every guide shows 3 to 5 stars in half steps: 3 for the build, plus half a star each for a description of at least 70 letters, notes on at least 3 skills, recommended stats and recommended gear. Hovering the stars opens a pop-up listing what is still missing. The editor shows the same stars live under the step tabs while you write; hover (or tap on a phone) to see how far each part is, e.g. คำอธิบาย 20/70 ตัวอักษร.
* **Top bar (user 2026-10-20):** **‹ ไกด์จากผู้เล่น** next to the logo goes back to the guide list. **เมนูไกด์** at the right holds รูปไกด์ (คัดลอกรูป, ดาวน์โหลดรูป) and เขียนไกด์ (เริ่มไกด์ใหม่, เขียนต่อ with your saved draft's name (shown only when there is one), ใช้ไกด์นี้เป็นต้นแบบ, นำเข้าโค้ดไกด์).
* **Title:** a row with the class icon, the server icon and `Lv.95`, then the guide name on its own line; under it the author in gold and the date.
* **Recommended stats:** one round dial per stat the author filled, in the author's order, the stat name under it in the stat's colour and the value in white (Prompt; user 2026-10-18). A number is a ring; a share (`70%`) or ตัน / ตาม is a potion flask in the stat's colour filled to its level (ตัน to the very top with a gold crown, ตาม a fifth with a slow wave and no bubbles), and the flasks fill from empty when the guide opens. A reader can click any value and type a number to try it: the hover timings follow at once (only numbers ever feed them), the dial becomes a glowing ring, and clearing it (or deleting until no digit is left, e.g. one Backspace on ตัน) brings the author's value (and the flask) back. Nothing a reader types is saved.
* **เซ็ตแนะนำ (recommended sets):** one row per set, numbered once at the left, the slots in game order as columns (no slot labels; the item name is the tooltip); a typed item's name is drawn as its icon. A Replica is a small icon on the item's bottom-right corner with a curved arrow up into the item, and the stat to put in shows under the icon as **ยัด** + the stat, in its colour. The PNG draws the same rows.
* **Hover:** a skill's name, then for a skill the author learned the timing lines (**คูลดาวน์** from AGI, **เวลาร่าย** from INT, the buff **ระยะเวลา** from CHA, each only if that stat is filled; LCK turns them into the same min-max range as skill-details).
